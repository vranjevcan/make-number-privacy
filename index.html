Privacy Policy — Rule Hunter

App: Rule Hunter
Package name: com.makenumber
Developer: Zoran Rajkov
Contact: zoranrajkov@gmail.com
Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android application Rule Hunter (package com.makenumber), published on Google Play by Zoran Rajkov, accesses, collects and shares.

Playing without an account

You can play Rule Hunter without signing in. If you do, the developer does not collect, store or receive any personal data from you, except the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Optional sign-in and the leaderboard

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app creates a sign-in account for you (using Firebase Authentication, a service of Google) and shows you on a public leaderboard. For this the developer stores:

  • a name to display: by default the name from your Google account, or a nickname you choose in Settings instead
  • a unique account ID generated by Firebase
  • your points: total, this week and the daily challenge

Your name and points are visible to every player who opens the leaderboard. The developer does not store or show your email address. Points you earn while not signed in are not sent anywhere and do not count on the leaderboard.

This data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and is kept until you delete it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and leaderboard data at any time:

  • in the app: Settings → Delete my data, or
  • by email: write to zoranrajkov@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you used — see how to request deletion.

Deletion removes your leaderboard entry and your sign-in account. Points stored on your own phone stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Usage statistics

Rule Hunter uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to see how the game is played. It records events such as which difficulty was chosen, which puzzle was solved or abandoned, and the points earned, together with an app-instance identifier created for the installation. It does not record your name or email. Google processes this data as described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of information locally, on your own phone, so it can work the way you expect:

  • your progress on each difficulty (which puzzle is next, and the number used to generate that puzzle's numbers)
  • your settings (sound volume, vibration on or off)
  • your points on this phone and the nickname you chose, if any
  • timestamps used to decide when an advertisement may be shown

This information stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Rule Hunter displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve and measure those advertisements, AdMob may access and process:

  • the Advertising ID of your device
  • device and network information (device model, operating system version, language, coarse location derived from IP address)
  • interaction with the advertisements themselves

The developer does not receive this data in identifiable form; only aggregate reports about the number of advertisements shown and revenue earned.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings, under Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

Rule Hunter is not directed at children under 16, and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Permissions

The app requests no dangerous Android permissions. It requires internet access so that advertisements can be loaded, and for sign-in and the leaderboard; the game itself works offline.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address, with a new "Last updated" date.
