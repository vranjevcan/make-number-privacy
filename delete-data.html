Delete your data — Rule Hunter

App: Rule Hunter
Package name: com.makenumber
Developer: Zoran Rajkov
Last updated: 17 October 2026

If you chose a nickname in Rule Hunter, you appear on the public leaderboard and you can ask for that data to be deleted. If you never chose a nickname, the developer holds no personal data about you and there is nothing to delete.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rule Hunter and tap the settings icon on the start screen.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

Your data is deleted right away. You can then choose a new nickname and start over as a new player.

Option 2 — by email

Write to zoranrajkov@gmail.com with the subject Rule Hunter - delete my data. Please include your nickname as it is shown on the leaderboard, and roughly when you last played, so the entry can be found. Your data is deleted within 30 days, normally much sooner.

If you used Sign in with Google in an earlier version of the app, write from the email address of that Google account.

What is deleted

  • your leaderboard entry: nickname, points (total, weekly, daily)
  • your anonymous account and its unique account ID (or, for earlier versions, your Google sign-in account)

What is not deleted

  • points and progress stored on your own phone — they stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data
  • anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), which are not linked to your name or email
