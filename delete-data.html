Delete your data — Rule Hunter

App: Rule Hunter
Package name: com.makenumber
Developer: Zoran Rajkov
Last updated: 14 October 2026

If you signed in to Rule Hunter with Google, you can ask for your account and leaderboard data to be deleted. If you never signed in, the developer holds no personal data about you and there is nothing to delete.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rule Hunter and tap the settings icon on the start screen.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.
  3. Sign in with Google once more when asked — this confirms the account is yours.

Option 2 — by email

Write to zoranrajkov@gmail.com with the subject Rule Hunter - delete my data, from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in. Please include the name shown on the leaderboard so the entry can be found. Your data is deleted within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What is deleted

  • your leaderboard entry: display name, points (total, weekly, daily)
  • your sign-in account and its unique account ID

What is not deleted

  • points and progress stored on your own phone — they stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data
  • anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), which are not linked to your name or email
