# Privacy Policy — Rule Hunter

**App:** Rule Hunter
**Package name:** com.makenumber
**Developer:** Zoran Rajkov
**Contact:** zoranrajkov@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 17 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android application **Rule Hunter**
(package `com.makenumber`), published on Google Play by **Zoran Rajkov**,
accesses, collects and shares.

## No sign-in

Rule Hunter does not ask you to sign in and does not ask for your email address
or any other account. When the app starts, it creates an **anonymous account**
for the installation (using Firebase Authentication, a service of Google). This
account has only a random ID and is not linked to your name, email or Google
account.

## The leaderboard

You appear on the public leaderboard only if you **choose a nickname**. For this
the developer stores:

- the nickname you chose
- the anonymous account ID generated by Firebase
- your points: total, this week and the daily challenge

Your **nickname and points are visible to every player** who opens the
leaderboard, so do not use your real full name if you do not want it to be
public. Until you choose a nickname, nothing is sent to the leaderboard; points
earned before that are kept on your phone and sent once you choose one.

This data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and is kept until you delete it.

Earlier versions of the app offered an optional **Sign in with Google**. If you
used it, your leaderboard entry and sign-in account stay stored until you delete
them as described below.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your leaderboard data at any time:

- in the app: **Settings → Delete my data**, or
- by email: write to zoranrajkov@gmail.com with your nickname — see
  [how to request deletion](https://vranjevcan.github.io/make-number-privacy/delete-data.html).

Deletion removes your nickname, your leaderboard entry and your anonymous
account. You can then choose a new nickname and start over as a new player.
Points stored on your own phone stay there until you uninstall the app or clear
its data.

## Usage statistics

Rule Hunter uses **Firebase Analytics** (Google) to see how the game is played.
It records events such as which difficulty was chosen, which puzzle was solved
or abandoned, and the points earned, together with an app-instance identifier
created for the installation. It does not record your name or email. Google
processes this data as described at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Data stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of information locally, on your own phone, so it
can work the way you expect:

- your progress on each difficulty (which puzzle is next, and the number used
  to generate that puzzle's numbers)
- your settings (sound volume, vibration on or off)
- your points on this phone and the nickname you chose, if any
- timestamps used to decide when an advertisement may be shown

This information stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app
or clear its data.

## Advertising

Rule Hunter displays advertisements through **Google AdMob**, a service provided
by Google. To serve and measure those advertisements, AdMob may access and
process:

- the **Advertising ID** of your device
- device and network information (device model, operating system version,
  language, coarse location derived from IP address)
- interaction with the advertisements themselves

The developer does not receive this data in identifiable form; only aggregate
reports about the number of advertisements shown and revenue earned.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings,
under **Settings → Google → Ads**.

## Children

Rule Hunter is not directed at children under 16, and does not knowingly collect
any data from them.

## Permissions

The app requests no dangerous Android permissions. It requires internet access
so that advertisements can be loaded, and for the leaderboard; the
game itself works offline.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same
